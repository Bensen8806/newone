The System is provided exclusively for the following purposes:

4. Responsibilities of Club Heads

Users with the Club Head role accept the following responsibilities when submitting a venue request:

  • All information provided in the request must be accurate and complete.

  • The Club Head is responsible for ensuring the venue is left in the same condition as found after the event.
  • Misrepresentation of event details to secure an approval may result in the request being cancelled and the user's access being suspended.

5. Approval Process

The approval of any venue request is at the sole discretion of the relevant HOD and the Principal. Submission of a request does not guarantee approval. Rejected requests may be resubmitted with corrections after addressing the reviewer's stated remark.

HODs and the Principal are required to provide a written remark for every decision (approval or rejection). Decisions made without a remark will not be accepted by the System.

6. Venue Conflicts

The System prevents double-booking by checking for overlapping approved events at the time of submission. Users must not attempt to circumvent this mechanism. Any conflict that arises due to incorrect information provided in a request is the responsibility of the submitting Club Head.

7. Certificates

Participation certificates are generated only for events that have been fully approved by both the HOD and the Principal. Certificates contain a unique verification code. Tampering with or falsifying a certificate is a serious violation and will be referred to the appropriate disciplinary authority.
